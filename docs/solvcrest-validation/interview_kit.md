# Solvcrest AI — Five-Interview Kit

**Objective:** Validate whether recurring production failures create a costly, unresolved problem and whether teams will sponsor a pilot focused on permanent root-cause remediation.

## Recommended interviews
1. SRE / platform engineer
2. Production support engineer
3. Java, Python, or API development lead
4. Engineering / SRE manager
5. Reliability / platform leader with buying influence

## Outreach message
Hello — I'm researching production incident investigation and permanent remediation for Solvcrest AI through Founder Institute. Could we schedule a 20–25 minute video interview? I'm interested in your real experiences, not making a sales pitch. No confidential incident data is needed. Would you be open to a conversation?

## Interview structure
- 0–3 min: Role, systems supported, consent
- 3–12 min: Last actual outage, tools used, time, workarounds
- 12–17 min: Repeat failures and permanent-fix barriers
- 17–21 min: Concept reaction, objections, alternatives
- 21–25 min: Budget owner, willingness to pay, and pilot commitment

## Consent
"With your permission, I'd like to record or transcribe this conversation solely to accurately summarize research findings. You can decline or stop recording at any time. Please do not share company-confidential data."

## Analysis prompt
"Analyze these five de-identified transcripts. Separate direct participant evidence from researcher assumptions. List the strongest pains, frequency and economic impact, current solutions, trust objections, buying authority, willingness to pay, and pilot commitments. Indicate the number of participants supporting each finding, cite transcript quotes, and provide a concise go/no-go recommendation. Do not fabricate missing answers."

Do not publish transcripts, interviewee identities, or confidential technical details in a public GitHub repository.
